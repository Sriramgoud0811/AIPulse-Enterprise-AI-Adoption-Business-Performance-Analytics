# 🚀 AIPulse: Enterprise AI Adoption & Business Performance Analytics

An end-to-end Data Analytics project that analyzes Enterprise AI Adoption and its impact on business performance, productivity, ROI, workforce transformation, and customer satisfaction using Python, SQL, Excel, and Power BI.

---

# 📖 Project Overview

Artificial Intelligence is transforming businesses across industries. Organizations are investing in AI tools, employee training, automation, and AI projects to improve productivity and business outcomes.

This project analyzes enterprise AI adoption data to understand how AI maturity, investment, training, and automation influence business performance indicators such as revenue growth, cost reduction, innovation, employee satisfaction, and customer satisfaction.

The project follows a complete Data Analytics workflow, including data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), SQL analysis, KPI development, dashboard creation, and business insights.

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze enterprise AI adoption across industries.
- Measure the relationship between AI adoption and business performance.
- Identify key factors affecting productivity and ROI.
- Build interactive dashboards for decision-making.
- Generate actionable business insights.
