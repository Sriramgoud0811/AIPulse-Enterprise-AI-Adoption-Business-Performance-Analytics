

# 🚀 AIPulse: Enterprise AI Adoption & Business Performance Analytics

## 📖 Project Overview

AIPulse is an end-to-end Data Analytics project that analyzes Enterprise AI Adoption and its impact on business performance, productivity, workforce transformation, operational efficiency, innovation, and return on investment (ROI). The project follows a complete analytics workflow, beginning with raw data collection and cleaning, followed by exploratory data analysis, SQL-based business analysis, KPI development, dashboard creation, and actionable business insights using Python, SQL, Excel, Power BI, Git, and GitHub.

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze enterprise AI adoption across different industries and countries.
* Measure the relationship between AI adoption and business performance.
* Identify factors affecting productivity, innovation, and ROI.
* Clean, validate, and transform raw datasets.
* Perform SQL-based business analysis.
* Develop KPIs for business decision-making.
* Build interactive Power BI dashboards.
* Generate meaningful business insights and recommendations.
